About Us

We Believe Every Woman Can

She Can Foundation is a government-registered Non-Governmental Organisation working tirelessly to uplift underprivileged women across India. Registered under the Indian Society Act, 1860 and affiliated with NITI Aayog, we operate with full transparency and governmental accountability.

Our work spans education, vocational training, health awareness, legal literacy, and community empowerment — touching lives from rural villages to urban slums across the length and breadth of India.

Our Programs

What We Do to Uplift Women

From education to employment, our programs are designed to create lasting change in the lives of underprivileged women across India.

📚

Education & Literacy

We run literacy programs, scholarship schemes, and educational support initiatives for women and girls from economically weaker sections. No woman should be denied the power of education.

Scholarships Adult Literacy Digital Skills
🪡

Vocational Training

Skill development workshops in tailoring, handicrafts, beauty & wellness, computer basics, and entrepreneurship — giving women sustainable livelihoods on their own terms.

Tailoring Handicrafts Entrepreneurship
🏥

Health & Wellness

Health camps, maternal care awareness, mental health workshops, and nutrition education programs conducted in rural and semi-urban areas to bring healthcare knowledge to every doorstep.

Health Camps Maternal Care Nutrition
⚖️

Legal Literacy

Empowering women with knowledge of their legal rights, domestic violence laws, property rights, and government schemes — because knowing your rights is the first step to exercising them.

Rights Awareness Legal Aid Govt Schemes
